Band history

Okean Elzy is one of the best-known Ukrainian rock bands. Its frontman and the author of most songs is Sviatoslav Vakarchuk.

  • 1994 The band is founded in Lviv. The first line-up: Sviatoslav Vakarchuk (vocals), Pavlo Hudimov (guitar), Yurii Khustochka (bass guitar) and Denys Hlinin (drums).
  • 1998 The musicians move to Kyiv and release the debut album "Там, де нас нема".
  • 2000 The second album "Янанебібув" is released.
  • 2001 The album "Модель" is released.
  • 2003 The album "Суперсиметрія" is released.
  • 2005 After changes in the line-up the band releases the album "GLORIA".
  • 2007 The album "Міра" is released.
  • 2010 The album "Dolce Vita" is released.
  • 2013 The album "Земля" is released.
  • 2014 The band celebrates its 20th anniversary with a stadium tour.
  • 2016 The album "Без меж" is released.
